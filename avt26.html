The Exercise

What is Avenger Triad?

Avenger Triad 2026 (AVT26) is an annual command post exercise led by U.S. Army Europe and Africa and NATO Allied Land Command. About 4,000 personnel, split evenly between U.S. forces and allies, worked from command posts in Germany, Poland, France and the Netherlands, with support from all 32 NATO nations. Rather than maneuvering troops, AVT26 tested how allied corps headquarters (V Corps, the 2nd Polish Corps, the 1st French Corps and the 1st German-Netherlands Corps) plan, share information and coordinate operations to defend NATO's eastern flank, across a scenario stretching from northern Norway to the Black Sea.
