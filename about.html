Who is Team JEDI?

Not a software vendor with a demo. A small group of former military logistics operators who built the pipe and the process to keep coalition data moving on its own.

How the partnership works

One product team, two companies.

Team JEDI isn't a prime contractor with a subcontractor bolted on. It's a joint venture between two organizations that each bring something the other doesn't have.

The Product

Nexus LCM

Nexus is where JEDI-X and JEDI MNLOGCOP actually get built. It's led by operators who lived the NATO LOGFAS problem firsthand — a Marine Corps logistics officer and a 26-year Italian Air Force LOGFAS instructor — not a software vendor hired after the fact to translate requirements they've never worked with.

The Delivery

Black & Rossi

Black & Rossi is a defense-sector solutions aggregator operating since 2002, led by a retired Marine Corps colonel, with its own independent track record in NATO data-interoperability work — including the alliance's unique-identification policy and a coalition logistics tool built for NSPA. They bring systems-integration reach that extends past what a single product team can cover alone.
